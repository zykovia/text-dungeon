warrior_f_idle_anim_f0.png, ranger_f_idle_anim_f0.png, cleric_f_idle_anim_f0.png,
and wizard_f_idle_anim_f0.png are recolors of that same pack's female knight,
elf, and wizzard idle frames (knight_f reused for both Warrior and Cleric),
produced with scripts/recolor_class_sprites.py to give each class a distinct
palette. As derivatives of CC0 art, they carry no additional license
restrictions.

wizard_f_idle_anim_f0.png, warrior_f_idle_anim_f0.png, and
cleric_f_idle_anim_f0.png also have hand-edited pixels beyond the palette
swap (a repainted face for the Wizard, a hair/ribbon accent for the Warrior,
a halo/wing accent for the Cleric), since a recolor alone can't change a
silhouette. See the comments next to each entry in
scripts/recolor_class_sprites.py.
